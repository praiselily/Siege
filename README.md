## What is Siege?

Siege is an automatic scan tool aimed mainly at Minecraft screenshares. 

Start a scan while the player's game is open. A few minutes later you have one organised report instead of downloading a dozen tools to get a picture of the suspect system.


<br>

## How it works

**1. Launch.** Run Siege on the player's PC. It asks for administrator rights on start.

**2. Unlock.** Enter your session key.

**3. Scan.** Wait 1-3 minutes for the scan to finish.

**4. Decide.** Read the findings, expand what matters and make your call.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>What do I need to run it?</b></summary>
<br>
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), administrator rights, and preferably the suspects game session.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it modify the player's PC or game?</b></summary>
<br>
No files or identifiers are left digitally or on disk.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is a detection an automatic verdict?</b></summary>
<br>
No. Siege gives you evidence and context, it can be helpful but will never replace manual checks
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How often is it updated?</b></summary>
<br>
Whenever I have time. New detections and improvements are added over time, and updates are announced in the Discord.
</details>
